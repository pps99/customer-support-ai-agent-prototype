# Shipping Policy

## Domestic Shipping (Singapore)
Orders placed before 2:00 PM SGT ship the same day. Standard shipping takes 2-4 business
days and costs S$4.90, or is free on orders over S$80. Express shipping (next business day)
costs S$9.90.

## International Shipping
We currently ship to Malaysia, Thailand, and Indonesia. International orders take 7-14
business days and cost S$19.90 flat rate. Customs duties, if any, are the responsibility of
the customer.

## Tracking
A tracking link is emailed automatically once a order is packed. Tracking numbers follow the
format SS-XXXXXXXX. If a tracking link has not arrived within 3 business days of ordering,
this is treated as a shipping delay (see Escalations policy).

## Delays
If a package has not moved for more than 5 business days according to the tracking page, or
has not arrived within 3 business days of the promised delivery window, the order qualifies
for an escalation to the logistics team. Support agents should not promise replacement
shipments or refunds for delayed packages without logistics team confirmation, since the
package may still be in transit.

## Address Changes
Shipping address changes can only be made before an order has entered "packed" status.
Address changes are a manual operation performed by a human agent after verifying the
requester's identity, because an incorrect address change could redirect a package to the
wrong person. This is never automated.
