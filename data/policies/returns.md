# Returns & Refunds Policy

## Eligibility
Shoes may be returned within 30 days of delivery if unworn, in original packaging, with tags
attached. Worn shoes may only be returned if there is a manufacturing defect.

## Standard Refund Process
1. Customer requests a return through support or the self-serve portal.
2. Customer ships the item back using the provided prepaid label.
3. Once the warehouse confirms receipt and condition, a refund is issued to the original
   payment method within 5-7 business days.

## Refunds Without a Return (Exceptions)
In rare cases (e.g. wrong item shipped, item lost by carrier, confirmed manufacturing
defect visible in a photo) a refund may be issued without requiring the item back. This
requires human review of evidence (photos, order history) and manager approval for any
refund over S$150. This is explicitly NOT something the support agent automates: refund
issuance always requires a human to authorize the transaction, because incorrect autonomous
refunds create direct financial loss and are difficult to reverse.

## Exchanges
Size and color exchanges follow the same eligibility rules as returns. Exchanges are
processed as a return + new order, not a direct swap.

## Final Sale Items
Items marked "Final Sale" at checkout, and all sock and insole purchases for hygiene
reasons, are not eligible for return or refund.
