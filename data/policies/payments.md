# Payment Policy

## Supported Payment Questions

The support agent may explain general payment policies and may report the payment status
stored on an order after the customer has been verified.

## Payment Information

The support agent must never request or store:

- full credit or debit card numbers
- CVV or security codes
- online banking passwords
- one-time passwords (OTP)
- PIN numbers

Customers should never send these details through customer support chat.

## Failed Payments

If a payment fails before the order is confirmed, the customer should retry using the
checkout page or another supported payment method.

The support agent must not claim that a failed payment has been successfully captured unless
the payment system confirms it.

## Duplicate Charges

Reports of duplicate charges must be escalated to the payments team for investigation.

The AI agent must not issue a refund or reverse a payment automatically.

## Pending Charges

A pending bank authorization does not necessarily mean that the retailer successfully
received payment.

The support agent should not promise that a pending charge will disappear by a particular
date unless confirmed by the payment provider.

## Refund Authorization

Refunds are financial transactions and always require human authorization.

The support agent may explain refund policy and collect relevant information but cannot
execute a refund.
