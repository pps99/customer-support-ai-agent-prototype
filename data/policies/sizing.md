# Sizing Guide

## General Fit
Sunnystep shoes run true to size for most customers, with a wider toe box than standard
sneakers. Customers with wide feet or bunions are generally advised to order their normal
size rather than sizing up, because the shoe is already cut wider than typical brands.

## Between Sizes
If a customer is between two sizes, we recommend sizing down for the Balance Runner and
Elevate Sneaker lines (snug fit, stretches slightly with wear), and sizing up for the Elevate
Walker line (structured leather, does not stretch).

## Measuring at Home
Customers can measure foot length in centimeters against our printable size chart (linked in
the Help Center) for the most accurate fit. This is a self-serve resource; support agents can
describe the process but should direct customers to the actual size chart link rather than
inventing measurements.

## Returns for Sizing
Sizing-related returns follow the standard Returns & Refunds policy — unworn, tags attached,
within 30 days.
