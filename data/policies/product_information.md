# Product Information Policy

## Approved Product Information

The support agent may answer questions about products only using information from the approved
product catalog or knowledge base.

Supported information may include:

- product name
- available sizes
- materials
- intended use
- fit guidance
- listed features
- care instructions
- currently published price

## Unknown Product Details

If a requested product detail is not present in the approved product data, the agent must not
guess or invent an answer.

The agent should state that the information could not be verified.

## Product Recommendations

The support agent may recommend products based on documented product characteristics and
customer-stated preferences.

Recommendations should not claim medical benefits unless those benefits are explicitly stated
in the approved product information.

## Availability

The agent must not claim that an item is in stock unless inventory data confirms availability.

## Pricing

The agent should use only the current price contained in the approved product catalog.

The agent must not invent discounts, coupon codes, promotional prices, or price-match policies.
