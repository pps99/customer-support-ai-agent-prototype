# Damaged, Defective, or Incorrect Item Policy

## Damaged Items

If a customer reports that footwear arrived damaged, the support agent should collect:

- the order number
- the email associated with the order
- a description of the damage
- clear photos showing the issue

The support agent must not determine compensation solely from the customer's text description.

## Manufacturing Defects

Possible manufacturing defects may be covered by the Warranty Policy.

Examples include:

- sole separation
- stitching failure
- insole breakdown under normal use

The product quality team must review warranty claims.

## Wrong Item Received

If the customer received a different product, size, or color from the confirmed order, the
case should be escalated for review.

The support agent may compare the order record with the customer's description but must not
automatically issue a replacement or refund.

## Missing Item

If an order contains multiple items and the customer reports that one is missing, the request
requires manual review.

## Resolution

Possible resolutions may include:

- replacement
- store credit
- refund
- return request

The AI agent must not promise a specific resolution before the appropriate team has reviewed
the case.
