# Warranty Policy

## Coverage
Sunnystep shoes carry a 6-month warranty against manufacturing defects: sole separation,
stitching failure, or insole breakdown under normal use. Warranty does not cover wear and
tear from normal use, damage from misuse, or cosmetic scuffing.

## Claims Process
Warranty claims require photos of the defect and the order number. Claims are reviewed by
the product quality team, not by the support agent directly. Support can collect the photos
and order number and open a claim, but cannot approve or deny a warranty claim, since that
requires physical inspection judgment the agent cannot make from a text description alone.

## Resolution
Approved warranty claims result in a free replacement pair (same style, same size) or store
credit if the style is discontinued. Resolution timing is typically 5-10 business days after
claim approval.
